Vesperblade

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Strong Bits, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile game Vesperblade (the "App"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information we collect

We don't require an account, and we don't collect your name, email, or any other directly-identifying information to play the game.

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads that keep Vesperblade free. AdMob and its ad partners may collect the following on our behalf:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. your device's advertising identifier) — linked to your device, used to serve and measure ads. This is only collected if you allow tracking when prompted by Apple's App Tracking Transparency system.
  • Advertising data and app interaction data — such as which ads were shown and how you interacted with them.
  • Coarse location — approximate location derived from your IP address, used for regional ad targeting.
  • Crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data — used to keep the App stable and the ad SDK functioning correctly. This data is not linked to your identity.

If you decline tracking permission, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized to you.

In-app purchases

Vesperblade offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. This purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit — we never see or store your payment information. We only receive confirmation of whether the purchase was completed, so we can turn off ads on your device.

Third parties

We use the following third-party service to operate the App:

  • Google AdMob — ad serving and measurement. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles data.

Your choices

You can allow or decline ad tracking at any time via your device's Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking, or when prompted in the App. You can also remove ads entirely (and the associated ad data collection) by purchasing Remove Ads.

Children's privacy

Vesperblade is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention

We don't operate our own servers that store player data. Any data collected by AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
