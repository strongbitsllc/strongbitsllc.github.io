Common questions

How do I remove ads?

From the title screen, tap "Remove Ads" and complete the one-time purchase. Ads will stop immediately and won't come back.

I paid to remove ads but I'm still seeing them. What do I do?

Tap "Restore" on the title screen — this re-checks your Apple ID's purchase history and re-applies the purchase without charging you again. If that doesn't resolve it, email us at the address above with the Apple ID email you purchased with.

I got a new phone / reinstalled the app. Will I lose my purchase?

No. Tap "Restore" on the title screen after reinstalling, and your Remove Ads purchase will be re-applied automatically.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple, not by us directly. Refunds are handled through Apple — you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
